# Research

## About Our Research Group

We are a group of young researchers focusing on:

- Tropical Climate - Indo-Pacific Warm Pool, Madden-Julian Oscillation, Tropical Cyclone, Convection

- Climate Change - Dynamics, Impacts, and Future Projection

- Extreme Weather & Climate Events - Temperature, Rainfall, Typhoon, etc.

- Climate Data Reconstruction

- Numerical Weather Prediction & AI Weather Prediction

- Data Science and Machine Learning Approaches in Atmospheric Science

Members:

- Qiuying Gan, Ph.D. Student, Sun Yat-sen University

- Guiling Ye, Ph.D. Student, Sun Yat-sen University

- Shengyuan Liu, Ph.D. Student, Guangdong Ocean University

- Weijian Luo, Ph.D. Student, Guangdong Ocean University

## Ongoing Research
1. Indo-Pacific Warm Pool expansion and climate impacts based on time-varied SST threshold for deep convection

2. Seasonality of the three-dimensional body of Tropical Warm Pool and its long-term variability

3. Climate snapshot simplification & Framework for quantifying variability in climate inhomogeneity and asymmetry

4. Spatial inhomogeneity and asymmetry of global surface temperature and precipitation change and extreme events under greenhouse warming

5. Long-term responses of Madden-Julian Oscillation and its seasonality to climate change in the 20th century

6. Reanalysis-Based Global Tropical Cyclone Tracks Dataset for the Twentieth Century (RGTrack-20C)

7. Typhoon/extreme weather predictability and precursor identification in NWP and AI weather models

8. Climatology, responses and impacts of quasi-biweekly-to-intraseasonal extreme weathers

9. And more . . . (Explore our [publication list](/publications))
